Shoulder & Knee Surgery

Expert surgical care for rotator cuff tears, ACL reconstruction, meniscus repair, labral tears, and complex shoulder and knee conditions.

Comprehensive Shoulder & Knee Surgical Care

The shoulder and knee are two of the most commonly injured joints, particularly among athletes and active individuals. Dr. Elguizaoui has extensive fellowship training in both shoulder and knee surgery, offering the full spectrum of treatment from conservative management to advanced surgical reconstruction.

Shoulder Conditions & Procedures

  • Rotator Cuff Repair — Arthroscopic repair of torn rotator cuff tendons to restore strength and function
  • Labral Repair (SLAP & Bankart) — Reattaching torn labral tissue to restore shoulder stability
  • Shoulder Stabilization — Surgical treatment for recurrent dislocations and instability
  • Shoulder Impingement Surgery — Subacromial decompression to create more space for tendons
  • AC Joint Reconstruction — Repair of the acromioclavicular joint after separation
  • Frozen Shoulder Release — Arthroscopic capsular release for adhesive capsulitis
  • Biceps Tendon Surgery — Tenodesis or tenotomy for biceps tendon pathology

Knee Conditions & Procedures

  • ACL Reconstruction — Rebuilding the anterior cruciate ligament using graft tissue
  • Meniscus Repair — Preserving the meniscus whenever possible through arthroscopic repair techniques
  • Meniscectomy — Partial removal of damaged meniscus tissue when repair is not possible
  • MCL and PCL Reconstruction — Repair of other knee ligaments
  • Patellar Stabilization — Surgical correction for recurring kneecap dislocations
  • Cartilage Restoration — Advanced techniques to repair or replace damaged cartilage
  • Knee Osteotomy — Realignment to redistribute weight and preserve the joint

Our Surgical Philosophy

Dr. Elguizaoui prefers arthroscopic and minimally invasive approaches whenever possible. These techniques result in less pain, smaller scars, and faster recovery. However, he is equally skilled in open surgical techniques when the situation demands it. Every treatment plan begins with a thorough evaluation and honest discussion about all available options, including non-surgical alternatives.

Common Symptoms

See Dr. Elguizaoui if you experience:

  • Joint pain that limits activity
  • Shoulder or knee instability
  • Catching, locking, or giving way
  • Swelling that doesn't resolve
  • Weakness in the arm or leg
