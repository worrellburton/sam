Specialty

Joint Preservation

Advanced techniques to protect and restore your natural joints, delaying or avoiding the need for joint replacement surgery.

Save Your Natural Joints with Advanced Techniques

Joint preservation is a rapidly evolving field focused on maintaining and restoring your body's natural joints rather than replacing them. Dr. Elguizaoui is deeply committed to this approach, having completed an international traveling fellowship through leading joint preservation clinics in Switzerland, the Netherlands, and Italy.

This philosophy is especially important for younger, active patients who develop arthritis or joint damage. By preserving the natural joint, patients can maintain a higher level of activity and delay or completely avoid the limitations that come with joint replacement.

Who Benefits from Joint Preservation?

  • Young and active patients with early arthritis
  • Athletes with cartilage damage or joint injuries
  • Patients with joint malalignment causing uneven wear
  • Those with labral tears in the hip or shoulder
  • Patients who want to delay or avoid joint replacement
  • Anyone with early-to-moderate joint degeneration

Joint Preservation Procedures

  • Cartilage repair and transplant — Restoring damaged cartilage surfaces using the latest techniques
  • Osteotomy — Realigning bones to redistribute weight and reduce stress on damaged areas
  • Meniscus repair and transplant — Preserving or replacing the knee's shock absorbers
  • Ligament reconstruction — Restoring joint stability to prevent further damage
  • Biologic injections — PRP and other regenerative therapies to stimulate natural healing
  • Arthroscopic debridement — Cleaning out damaged tissue to reduce pain and improve function

Preservation vs. Replacement

Joint preservation aims to:

  • Maintain your natural anatomy
  • Allow higher activity levels
  • Avoid implant limitations
  • Delay or prevent replacement surgery
