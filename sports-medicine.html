Specialty

Sports Medicine

Comprehensive treatment of athletic injuries with a focus on getting you back in the game. Dr. Elguizaoui brings fellowship training and professional sports experience to every patient.

Expert Sports Injury Care in New York City

Dr. Elguizaoui completed his sports medicine fellowship at Lenox Hill Hospital, one of the most prestigious sports medicine programs in the country. During his fellowship, he provided care for athletes from the New York Jets (NFL) and New York Islanders (NHL), giving him firsthand experience with elite-level sports injuries and recovery protocols.

Whether you're a professional athlete, weekend warrior, or someone who simply wants to stay active, Dr. Elguizaoui applies the same world-class expertise to every patient. His approach combines advanced surgical techniques with conservative management strategies to achieve the best possible outcomes.

Conditions Treated

  • ACL tears and ligament injuries
  • Meniscus tears
  • Rotator cuff tears and shoulder injuries
  • Labral tears (shoulder and hip)
  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Muscle strains and tendon injuries
  • Stress fractures
  • Tennis and golfer's elbow
  • Runner's knee and IT band syndrome
  • Concussion management

Our Approach

Dr. Elguizaoui takes a comprehensive approach to sports medicine that prioritizes returning you to your sport or activity as safely and quickly as possible. This includes:

  • Accurate diagnosis using advanced imaging and clinical evaluation
  • Conservative treatment first including physical therapy, bracing, and activity modification
  • Regenerative therapies such as PRP injections for accelerated healing
  • Minimally invasive surgery when needed, using arthroscopic techniques for faster recovery
  • Sport-specific rehabilitation programs designed around your goals
