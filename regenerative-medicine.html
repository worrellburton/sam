Specialty

Regenerative Medicine

Biologic alternatives to surgery including PRP therapy and other regenerative treatments for arthritis and soft tissue injuries.

Heal Naturally with Biologic Treatments

Dr. Elguizaoui is a strong advocate for biologic alternatives to surgery and regenerative medicine. These cutting-edge treatments harness your body's own healing mechanisms to repair damaged tissues, reduce inflammation, and relieve pain — often without the need for surgery.

Regenerative medicine is particularly valuable for young, active patients who develop arthritis or tendon problems and want to avoid or delay surgical intervention. Dr. Elguizaoui's training in Switzerland, the Netherlands, and Italy exposed him to the latest European advances in biologic and regenerative therapies.

Regenerative Treatments Offered

  • Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy — Concentrated platelets from your own blood are injected into the injured area to accelerate healing and reduce inflammation
  • Hyaluronic Acid Injections — Viscosupplementation that restores joint lubrication and cushioning for arthritis relief
  • Corticosteroid Injections — Targeted anti-inflammatory injections for acute pain relief
  • Biologic Augmentation — Using biologic materials to enhance surgical repair and improve healing outcomes

Conditions Treated

  • Early to moderate osteoarthritis
  • Tendinitis and tendinopathy (tennis elbow, Achilles tendon, etc.)
  • Partial rotator cuff tears
  • Meniscus injuries
  • Ligament sprains
  • Muscle strains
  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Chronic joint pain

Benefits of Regenerative Medicine

  • Non-surgical, in-office procedures
  • Minimal downtime and quick recovery
  • Uses your body's own healing abilities
  • Can delay or avoid the need for surgery
  • May be combined with other treatments for optimal results
  • Low risk of allergic reaction (using your own blood products)

Conservative Care First

Dr. Elguizaoui believes in exhausting non-surgical options before recommending surgery. Regenerative medicine is a key part of this approach.

PRP Therapy FAQ

  • In-office procedure (30-60 minutes)
  • Uses your own blood
  • Minimal pain and no incisions
  • Return to activities within days
  • Results improve over weeks
