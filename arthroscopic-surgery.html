Specialty

Arthroscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive surgical techniques that mean smaller incisions, less pain, and faster recovery for shoulder and knee conditions.

Minimally Invasive Surgery for Better Outcomes

Arthroscopic surgery is a minimally invasive procedure where a small camera (arthroscope) and specialized instruments are inserted through tiny incisions, typically less than 1 cm each. This approach allows Dr. Elguizaoui to diagnose and treat joint problems with significantly less tissue disruption than traditional open surgery.

Dr. Elguizaoui is fellowship-trained in advanced arthroscopic techniques, having completed his sports medicine fellowship at Lenox Hill Hospital, a nationally recognized center for arthroscopic innovation. His international training in Switzerland, the Netherlands, and Italy further expanded his expertise in cutting-edge minimally invasive approaches.

Benefits of Arthroscopic Surgery

  • Smaller incisions (typically less than 1 cm)
  • Less post-operative pain
  • Reduced risk of infection
  • Faster recovery and return to activities
  • Less scarring
  • Often performed as outpatient surgery
  • More precise visualization of joint structures

Conditions Treated Arthroscopically

Shoulder

  • Rotator cuff tears
  • Labral tears (SLAP lesions)
  • Shoulder instability and dislocations
  • Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis)
  • Shoulder impingement
  • AC joint problems

Knee

  • ACL reconstruction
  • Meniscus tears (repair or trimming)
  • Cartilage damage
  • Loose bodies
  • Synovitis
  • Patellar problems

What to Expect

Most arthroscopic procedures are performed on an outpatient basis, meaning you go home the same day. The surgery is typically done under regional or general anesthesia. Recovery times vary depending on the specific procedure, but most patients experience significantly faster recovery compared to open surgery. Dr. Elguizaoui will develop a personalized rehabilitation plan to help you return to your activities as quickly and safely as possible.
